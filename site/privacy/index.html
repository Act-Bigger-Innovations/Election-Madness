Election Madness 2028

Privacy

Effective November 26, 2026

Election Madness 2028 is a fan game run by Andrew Sartain as Mr. Earth Rebirth. It is not a poll, a campaign or a voter registration service. There are no accounts. This page says what the site keeps.

What we collect

  • Votes on this site: the matchup, your pick, and a random code your browser makes up so one device counts once. The code is not tied to your name.
  • "I registered" taps: the state you picked and the same random code. We cannot see whether you actually registered, and we never see what you enter on a state's website.
  • Email, if you sign up for the weekly matchup: your email address.
  • Votes on Instagram: comments are public on Instagram. We count them by hand and publish totals, not names.

We do not ask for your name, party, address, date of birth or anything about how you are registered.

How we use it

To count votes, show totals, and send the weekly email to people who asked for it. We publish counts only.

Who we share it with

The companies that run the site for us: Supabase (database, United States) and Cloudflare (web hosting). Links to vote.gov, state election sites, Power the Polls and Instagram take you to their sites under their own policies.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not give it to any candidate, campaign, party or committee.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Clearing your browser's site data removes the random code from your device. To have a vote, a registration tap or your email removed from our records, write to andrew@earthrebirthnow.com; we answer within 30 days.

Children

The site is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: andrew@earthrebirthnow.com.
