How it works

1 · Your comment is your vote

Comment one name from the group plus a reason on that group's Instagram post. One counted comment per account. Voting runs Monday 10 AM to Sunday 9 PM CT.

2 · One survives

The name with the biggest share of counted comments wins the group and qualifies. Runners-up go to the bubble race for four wildcards per side.

3 · Lock Watch

When someone formally announces a 2028 run they get a LOCKED tag. If they rule it out, they're marked OUT. When the real fields are set, Season 1 runs on the 2028 primary calendar.

Counting rules. Accounts under 30 days old or with no posts don't count. A group with a sudden flood of comments is reviewed before it's final. Ties go to the most-liked argument.

Corrections. If we get a fact wrong, we fix the card and pin the correction.

This is a fan game, not a poll. The score measures who shows up to argue. Election Madness is not affiliated with any party, campaign, or the NCAA.
